# ELEMENT-camping-platform

This was a fun personal project I did parallel to my studies. It is designed to be made with a CNC. If this is not a tool you have access to I assume you could do it with a jig saw and some patience. 

Feel free to use and tweak the file as you want. **Please keep it non-commercial and don't charge anyone for it.** If you build it, I would love to see photos! --> email me: bastian.luchs@gmail.com

**Fabrication & CAM Notes:** 

The CAM is set up for the ShopBot we had in school. Here are some notes from my build process:

* **Plywood:** I used 2 4x8 sheets of 3/4" Baltic birch. 
* **Parameters:** There are only 2 parameters. **PLYWOOD** should be the exact thickness of your stock. **TOLERANCE** is the extra space for joints; I used 0.01".
* **Bits:** I used communal bits from school, but if I did it again, I’d use a 1/4" compression finishing bit for better edges and less sanding.
* **Tabs:** I set the tabs randomly and didn't account for grain direction, which caused some tear-out when removing them. Think about this.
* **Finish:** I used Polyurethane, but didn't account for it in my tolerances, so I had to sand more. I’d suggest an oil-based finish or a tad more generous tolerances next time.

**Hardware & Assembly**

* **Hinges:** I used two 20-inch piano hinges and heavy-duty locking hinges for the feet. Both from amazon but I encourage you to buy them at your local hardware store.
* **Support:** The rear sides (where the trunk side pockets are) aren't supported so I could reach the pockets; I used wooden offcuts and bungees for a temporary forever fix. Maybe you can find a more elegant solution.
* **Storage:** Designed to fit one regular and one rectangular US milk crate on each side. Skis or fishing rods fit in the middle.
* **Mattress:** I used a 3"full-size mattress topper from Target.

**Limits**

* **Coolers:** A big cooler won't fit comfortably unless it's in the passenger seat. I used a small one instead.
